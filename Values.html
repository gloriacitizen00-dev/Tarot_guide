Our Values

Ethical principles guiding the development of Maneko Neki Tarot

1. Human-Centered Design

Maneko Neki Tarot is built with respect for human creativity, intuition, and emotional experience. The system is designed to support reflection, not replace human judgment or personal decision-making.

2. Responsible Use of AI

We acknowledge that artificial intelligence must be developed with care. This project promotes responsible, transparent, and ethical use of AI systems, ensuring that technology serves the user in a safe and meaningful way.

3. Transparency

Users should understand that tarot readings are generated through a combination of AI interpretation, symbolic systems, and contextual data. The goal is clarity, not illusion.

4. Privacy & Respect

User interaction is treated with respect and care. The system is designed to avoid unnecessary data collection and to prioritize user trust and safety.

5. Learning & Improvement

The AI system evolves over time, but always under controlled and ethical boundaries. Improvement is focused on enhancing user experience, not manipulating behavior.

6. Balance Between Technology & Mystery

While AI powers the system, the essence of tarot remains symbolic and interpretative. We preserve the mystery and cultural depth of tarot without reducing it to pure data analysis.
