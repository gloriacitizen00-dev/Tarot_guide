About Maneko Neki Tarot

Our Story

Maneko Neki Tarot was born as a university capstone project. The original goal was to develop an artificial intelligence system capable of recognizing and distinguishing images. While this fulfilled the technical requirements of the assignment, we wanted to create something more meaningful—a complete experience where technology could support the art of tarot reading.

Our Vision

Our vision was to build an AI capable of doing much more than identifying tarot cards. By learning the symbolism and meanings behind each card, the system continues to improve its ability to deliver insightful, thoughtful, and personalized tarot readings. Rather than replacing intuition, our goal is to create a companion that enriches the user's journey of self-reflection.

AI Meets Tarot

As the project evolved, so did its capabilities. Today, Maneko Neki Tarot also considers contextual information such as the current weather and the phase of the moon, allowing every reading to become more unique and connected to the present moment. By combining artificial intelligence with tarot symbolism and environmental data, every experience becomes richer and more personalized.

Why Cats?

The choice of cats was never accidental. Throughout history, cats have been associated with mystery, intuition, protection, and magic in cultures around the world. They represent wisdom, curiosity, independence, and quiet companionship—all qualities that perfectly embody the spirit of Maneko Neki Tarot. Our feline guides invite users to explore the unknown with confidence and an open mind.

The Meaning Behind Our Name

The name Maneko Neki Tarot is inspired by the traditional Japanese Maneki-neko, also known as the "Beckoning Cat." A timeless symbol of prosperity, fortune, and welcoming energy, its raised paw represents an invitation to begin a new journey. That invitation reflects exactly what our project hopes to offer: a warm and magical experience where ancient tarot wisdom meets modern artificial intelligence.

Looking Ahead

What began as a university assignment has grown into an ongoing passion project. We continue exploring new ways to combine technology, creativity, and symbolism to create a unique tarot experience that is engaging, meaningful, and constantly evolving.
