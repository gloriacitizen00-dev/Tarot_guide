Tarot Game Frequently Asked Questions

Discover how the SkyCortex OS Tarot Game works, how to play, and what makes this interactive Maneki-neko inspired experience unique.

What is the Tarot Game?

The Tarot Game is an interactive digital tarot experience inspired by the Japanese Maneki-neko lucky cat. It combines tarot symbolism, storytelling, illustration, and technology to create a unique browser-based experience.

Do I need to download anything to play?

No. The Tarot Game is a browser-based interactive experience. You can play directly online using your web browser without downloading or installing any application.

Is this a real tarot reading?

The Tarot Game is designed as a creative and reflective experience using tarot symbolism and storytelling elements. It is created for entertainment, imagination, and personal reflection rather than predicting the future.

How does the game work?

Choose your deck, begin your reading, and allow the system to generate your tarot cards. Each card includes its own symbolism, meanings, and interpretations to create a unique experience every time.

How many tarot spreads are available?

The Tarot Game currently includes six different spreads, each designed to provide a unique reading experience and different types of guidance.

The game was created with a reading engine that generates a final interpretation based on the cards selected, their meanings, and the chosen spread.

Each spread can provide different insights, including personal reflection, love themes, moon phases, career opportunities, cosmic connections, and other symbolic messages designed to make every reading unique.

What tarot decks are available?

The Tarot Game currently includes different deck options:

• Major Arcana
• Minor Arcana
• Mixed Deck

Each option creates a different reading experience.

What are Rituals?

Rituals is a creative section inside the Tarot Game where you can explore symbolic practices inspired by energy, reflection, nature, and cosmic themes.

You can access different rituals by selecting the Welcome to Rituals button and choosing the diamond you would like to explore.

Each diamond contains different experiences:

🌙 Lunar Diamond – Beginner rituals focused on simple practices and personal reflection.

☀️ Solar Diamond – Intermediate rituals designed for deeper exploration.

✨ Cosmic Diamond – More advanced and extensive rituals connected with larger symbolic experiences.

What is the Northern Light Research Station?

The Northern Light Research Station is a tool created to enhance the Tarot Game experience by connecting readings with real-time sky information.

The station connects with NOAA space weather data to provide information about current solar conditions and cosmic activity.

The information is updated according to NOAA's available updates, allowing the experience to incorporate real astronomical data into the creative reading environment.

What makes this tarot experience different?

This project combines traditional tarot concepts with front-end development, UX design, creative coding, and storytelling to transform a classic idea into an interactive digital experience.

What is the Moon Calendar?

The Moon Calendar is a visual system based on the natural lunar cycle, which lasts approximately 29.53 days.

It is designed to provide a symbolic and visual representation of the Moon's cycle by combining astronomical information with a clean and accessible design.

The calendar can be used for visualization, reflection, and creative or thematic projects inspired by lunar cycles and Earth's natural satellite.

How does the Moon Calendar work?

The Moon Calendar allows you to explore different months and discover the lunar information available for each period.

When you select a month, the calendar updates to display the corresponding lunar phases and events.

The Moon Summary section provides information about important moon phases and their dates.

The Lunar Cycle visualization uses a sparkle indicator to highlight the most recent moon phase and show the progression of the cycle.

The daily calendar displays the moon name, moon age, and illumination percentage for each day.

What are Moon Phases?

Moon Phases represent the different stages of the Moon's appearance during its natural cycle around Earth.

This system tracks whether the Moon is:

🌑 New Moon
🌒 Waxing Moon
🌕 Full Moon
🌘 Waning Moon

The Moon Phase system provides a live visualization combining astronomical accuracy with a simple and accessible interface.

It is designed for learning, visualization, reflection, and creative projects inspired by lunar astronomy.

What is the Cosmic Calendar?

The Cosmic Calendar is a hybrid astronomical and symbolic calendar that combines real sky events with creative interpretations.

It maps different celestial phenomena throughout the year, including:

✨ Meteor showers
🌑 Eclipses
🌌 Aurora events
🌙 Lunar phenomena

Select the month you would like to explore and discover the different sky events available during that period.
