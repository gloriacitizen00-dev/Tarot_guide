The mind behind Maneko Neki Tarot

Creator Profile

Gloria Martínez

AI Developer · UX Designer · Concept Creator

Maneko Neki Tarot began as a university capstone project focused on artificial intelligence and image recognition systems. The original goal was to train a system capable of identifying and classifying visual inputs.

Over time, the project evolved into a more ambitious creative system that combines AI, tarot symbolism, and contextual data such as lunar phases and environmental conditions to generate personalized readings.

This project reflects a fusion of machine learning concepts, interactive design, and narrative systems, aiming to create an experience that is both technological and intuitive.

The goal was not only to build an AI system, but to design a meaningful digital experience that connects users with symbolism, reflection, and interpretation.
